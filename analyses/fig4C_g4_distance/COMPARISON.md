## Notes

- Order matters: the published procedure finds the closest G4 among ALL predictions and then discards hotspots whose closest G4 is weak (|score| <= 1.2) or large (> 200 bp), instead of re-matching them to the closest strong G4. Filtering G4s first keeps every hotspot; the median stays 0 but the IQR widens. Suggest describing/using the prefiltered version.
- The manuscript reports no background expectation. The permutation null above (random 10 bp bins, same chromosomes, blacklist removed) gives that reference; random bins are not GC-matched, and G4s are GC-rich, so part of the excess is expected from the GC correlation in Fig. 4B.
- 'Interquartile distance' in the manuscript is the IQR of the signed distances.
